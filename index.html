Notes on the Amex Platinum

  • FHR Fine Hotels + Resorts (Reid's, Savoy Palace, The Reserve). Usual perks: upgrade when available, daily breakfast for 2, noon check-in when available, guaranteed 4pm checkout, a property credit (often ~$100) and Wi-Fi.
  • THC The Hotel Collection (Saccharum). Perks need a 2+ night stay: an upgrade when available and a property credit. Breakfast and late checkout usually aren't included. If you use the complimentary 4th night, check when booking that it covers Jan 9–13 and that you still get the Hotel Collection perks with it, because promotional rates sometimes can't be combined.
  • Your Platinum hotel credit applies to prepaid FHR/THC bookings through Amex Travel and resets every half-year (Jan–Jun / Jul–Dec). If you book one Amex hotel in Dec 2026 and another in Jan 2027, you may be able to use two credits. Check the terms in your Amex account first.
  • Weather: in January the south coast (Calheta, Ponta do Sol, Funchal) is the sunniest and driest. The north coast and the mountains are wetter, which is why they're so green. The coast is about 17–20°C and the peaks are near freezing.

III

Day by Day

Your picks show up on each day, sorted by drive time from that night's hotel. Choose one per day, and save the views for a clear day. Dinner ideas are all in one list in section IV.

Fits your picks onto the days with the least total driving. It keeps your first resort day lazy, pairs picks that start close together, and uses the drive between hotels. You can change any day afterward.

IV

Where to Eat

No need to plan dinners ahead. Every restaurant is listed under whichever of your hotels it's closer to, nearest first. Tap a name to open it in Google Maps, and remember your hotel's own restaurants too.
